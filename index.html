Our story

A legacy of luxury, reimagined daily

Enter a world of contemporary hair cutting, colouring and styling, guided by an experienced team of professionals. Studie'o7 was founded by Mr. Rajesh, who leads the studio in bringing the latest trends and techniques to every chair, ensuring each look is crafted to perfection.

Since then, Studie'o7 has grown to 200+ salons across 80+ cities and 10+ states, with 50+ franchises spanning Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Kerala.

Our aim stays simple — to make every customer feel and look beautiful. We offer high-end, premium services from head to toe, including advanced skin solutions, hair designing and much more.
